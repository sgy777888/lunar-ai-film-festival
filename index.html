About LAIFF

An Avant-Garde Online Moving-Image Festival

Lunar AI Film Festival (LAIFF) is an avant-garde online moving-image festival for global creators. Our core vision is ambitious: in the future, we will host an on-site screening event on the Moon, showcasing all the official selections of this edition.

We welcome human-AI collaborative works worldwide, including narrative features, shorts, animation, documentary, experimental film, interactive media and AI screenplays. At present, we adopt remote jury review and cloud screening to break geographical barriers. We invite creators to explore the boundary between reality and virtuality and redefine cinematic expression. Original storytelling and critical thinking are valued above technical spectacle. We build a long-term path for AI filmmakers toward an off-world screening.

我们采用远程评审与云端展映，打破地域限制；相比技术奇观，我们更看重原创叙事与批判性思考，并为 AI 电影人铺设一条通往地外放映的长期路径。
